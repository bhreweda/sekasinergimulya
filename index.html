End-to-End Assurance

4-Step Quality Control & Lab Testing

Inspected by SGS / Sucofindo prior to Bill of Lading
01

Raw Material Screening

Incoming sawdust and wood chips undergo bark-content auditing, metal/sand separation, and initial moisture profiling.

02

Drying & Hammer Milling

Rotary drum dryers reduce moisture to a uniform 8–10% before fine hammer milling ensures homogeneous particle size.

03

Pelletizing & Cooling

High-pressure ring-die extrusion activates natural wood lignin, followed by counter-flow cooling and vibrating dust sieving (< 0.5% fines).

04

SGS / Sucofindo Inspection

Independent third-party laboratory sampling verifies calorific value, ash, sulfur, and moisture before issuing Certificate of Analysis (COA).
